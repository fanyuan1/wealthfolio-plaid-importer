# 0057 — D56: Joint accounts

## Context
Both copies hold the same activity, so syncing both counts it twice (2026-11-08)

## Decision
Select all turns on one copy of a joint account linked under several owners (same institution, mask and subtype). Accounts warns when both copies are on Plaid has no joint flag: Identity owners (when linked), or a joint word in the account's own name, say so.

## Consequences
None recorded beyond the decision.
